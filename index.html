Zomato AI Data Pipeline: Warehouse & Review Intelligence

Food-delivery platforms sit on millions of orders and hundreds of thousands of free-text reviews, but turning that into a trustworthy answer still means hand-written SQL or reading reviews one at a time. This pipeline automates it — raw CSVs flow through a medallion-architecture Snowflake warehouse (Supabase → Snowflake → dbt), rebuilt daily by a single Airflow DAG, with an LLM enrichment stage that turns every review into structured sentiment and topic data as it lands. A RAG layer and a schema-grounded text-to-SQL chat then let anyone ask the warehouse a question in plain English — or ask what customers are actually complaining about — and get a real, cited answer instead of writing a query or reading reviews by hand.

A citation-grounded chat layer lets engineers just ask what changed instead of comparing two drawings by hand.
